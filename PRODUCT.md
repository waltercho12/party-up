# Party-up Product Specification

Version: 1.0

## 1. Vision

Create a gaming culture where anyone can easily find trustworthy people to play with.
Regardless of age, gender, location, or game popularity,
everyone deserves a great teammate.

## 2. Mission

Help players find trustworthy teammates within five minutes.

Party-up is not designed to maximize the number of matches.
It is designed to maximize meaningful gaming experiences.

## 3. Product Definition

Party-up is not a game matching service.
Party-up is a trust-based social platform for gamers.

The product exists to answer one fundamental question:
"Who do I genuinely want to play with again?"

Games are simply the shared environment where trust is built.

People are the product.
Games are the content.

## 4. Core Philosophy

Trust is built through consistent behavior, not status.
Good experiences create lasting relationships.
Competition is optional.
Respect is mandatory.

Every implementation should reinforce these principles.

## 5. Target Users

Party-up is designed for players who:

- Regularly play multiplayer games alone.
- Value personality over skill.
- Prefer respectful teammates over high-ranked teammates.
- Want long-term gaming relationships.
- Spend too much time searching for trustworthy players.

## 6. Problems We Solve

Modern gaming communities often suffer from:

- Anonymous toxicity.
- Disposable one-time interactions.
- Rank-first matchmaking.
- Poor trust signals.
- Unsafe LFG communities.
- Time wasted searching for good teammates.

Party-up exists to solve these problems.

## 7. Behavior Design

Every feature should encourage healthy player behavior.

Party-up encourages players to:

- Meet new people.
- Play respectfully.
- Communicate positively.
- Replay with great teammates.
- Help new players.
- Build long-term gaming friendships.
- Respect every game equally.

Features exist to shape behavior.
Behavior does not exist to justify features.

## 8. Product Principles

When designing features:

- Prioritize trust over functionality.
- Prioritize simplicity over complexity.
- Prioritize long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.
- Prefer structured system selections (official game lists, predefined tags, etc.) over free-text input whenever possible.
- Free-text should complement structured data, not replace it.
- Keep user decisions simple.
- Reduce unnecessary friction.

Simple systems are preferred whenever they achieve the same user outcome.

## 9. Trust & Reputation

Reputation measures one thing:
"Would you genuinely play with this person again?"

Reputation is never based on:

- Rank
- Skill
- Win rate
- Playtime
- Popularity
- Followers

Trust is earned through consistent behavior.
The reputation algorithm remains private.
Users should understand good behavior,
not optimize for the algorithm.

## 10. Experience Philosophy

Good experiences are the reward.

Party-up intentionally avoids:

- Daily login rewards
- XP systems
- Attendance bonuses
- Artificial engagement loops
- Endless progression systems
- Reward addiction mechanics

Players should return because they enjoyed playing,
not because they fear missing rewards.

## 11. Game Philosophy

Every game deserves respect.

Party-up supports competitive, casual, co-op, indie, sandbox, survival, MMORPG, and future games equally.
Game popularity should never determine product quality.

## 12. Trust, Safety & Privacy

Privacy builds trust.

Party-up collects only the minimum information necessary.
Gender is never exposed to other users and never influences the product experience.
Moderation is evidence-based.
Harassment, discrimination, and public shaming are never tolerated.
Players always retain the ability to block unwanted interactions.
Internal trust calculations, abuse detection rules, moderation thresholds, and reputation algorithms remain private.

## 13. Success Metrics

Success is measured by relationship quality rather than engagement quantity.

### North Star Metric

Completed parties where every participant finishes the reputation evaluation.

### Supporting Metrics

- Party completion rate
- Evaluation completion rate
- Replay rate
- 30-day retention
- Percentage of players who find a party within five minutes
- Report resolution time
- Support satisfaction

## 14. What Party-up Will Never Become

Party-up will never become:

- A dating service.
- A gender-based matching platform.
- A popularity contest.
- A follower-driven social network.
- A leaderboard platform.
- A skill-first matchmaking platform.
- A reward addiction platform.
- A playtime farming platform.
- A platform where toxicity is tolerated for growth.

Protecting this philosophy always takes precedence over adding new features.
When future product decisions conflict with these principles,
the principles should win.
